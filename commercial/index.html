Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff during the rush. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Scranton

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched quickly, anywhere in Scranton and the surrounding area.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
